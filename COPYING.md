## 📝 How to Comply When Copying

If you copy, fork, or redistribute FED Play:

1. Keep the **`LICENSE`** file intact and visible.
2. Preserve the **copyright notice** (the line in `LICENSE` and in
   [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).
3. If you modify files substantially, you may add your own copyright line to
   the notice; do not remove the original.
4. If you redistribute third-party assets yourself (rather than referencing
   them), respect their respective licenses — attribution where required.
5. If you rebrand, update references to your own PayPal/Stripe public client
   IDs in `layout.js` and your own support/Ko-fi links. **Never** add secret
   keys to the repo. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
